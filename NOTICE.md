# Notice

AetherLink X contains two first-party areas: the Android product experience and
the ALX authenticated transport implementation. ALX Preview 8 uses standard
TLS 1.3 and QUIC cryptography; it does not claim a new encryption algorithm.

The compatibility path incorporates or builds against third-party projects,
including Xray-core, hev-socks5-tunnel, quic-go and uTLS. Those components
remain under their respective licenses. Android attribution details are listed
in client/android/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
